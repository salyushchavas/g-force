## Originals are not committed

The full-quality originals and the graded masters are not committed to this repository. Only the web encodes under `public/media/` are part of the site.

- **Re-downloading:** use the links above (Pexels "UHD" renditions; Pixabay "large"; Unsplash full size in sRGB, `fm=jpg&q=85`).
- **Settings to reproduce the edits:** the "Notes" column.

## Encoding reference

- **Video:**
  - Each service loop is 960×540, 25 fps, with no audio track.
  - H.264 High, yuv420p, BT.709, `+faststart`, CRF 23–25 (x264 `veryslow`, `aq-mode=3`).
  - VP9 two-pass, CRF 31–33.
  - Every file is under 900 KB (VMAF 91–96 against the graded master).
  - Loops are frame-exact cross-dissolves.
- **Posters:** 1280×720, taken from the same grade, in AVIF, WebP and JPG.
- **Stills:**
  - 16:9 desktop crops at 640, 1280 and 1920 px (learner capped at 1600 px).
  - 4:5 mobile crops at 800×1000.
  - AVIF is 10-bit `libaom-av1 -still-picture 1 -crf 22`. That setting matches the JPG on VMAF at about a third of the bytes.
  - WebP is q72. JPG is `q:v 3`.
- **Replacing a file:** `/media/` is served `immutable` for a year (`public/.htaccess`). Give any replacement a new file name and update `media.json`.
